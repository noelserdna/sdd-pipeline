# Customer Needs

> **Project:** todo-app
> **Captured by:** requirements engineer, interview with the product owner
> **Last updated:** 2026-08-24

The customer's own words, captured before any requirement was written and read back to them. Requirements cite these IDs in their `Needs:` line.

### N-001: Jot a task down without leaving the terminal
- **Quote:** "I live in the terminal and I keep forgetting small things. I want to write a task down in one command, without opening anything else."
- **Who:** Laura Gómez, product owner
- **When:** 2026-08-20
- **Status:** confirmed

### N-002: See what is left and what is done
- **Quote:** "I need to see at a glance what is still pending and what I already finished. When the list gets long, I only want to see what is left."
- **Who:** Laura Gómez, product owner
- **When:** 2026-08-20
- **Status:** confirmed

### N-003: Tick things off and throw away mistakes
- **Quote:** "Ticking things off is the satisfying part. And sometimes I add something by mistake, so I need to get rid of it."
- **Who:** Laura Gómez, product owner
- **When:** 2026-08-20
- **Status:** confirmed

### N-004: The list survives closing the terminal
- **Quote:** "If I close the terminal and my list is gone, the tool is useless to me."
- **Who:** Laura Gómez, product owner
- **When:** 2026-08-20
- **Status:** confirmed

### N-005: It answers instantly and keeps working after updates
- **Quote:** "I'll run it dozens of times a day, so it has to answer instantly. And the last tool I used broke after an update; when you add things later, don't break what already works."
- **Who:** Laura Gómez, product owner
- **When:** 2026-08-20
- **Status:** confirmed

### N-006: Check the list from my phone
- **Quote:** "It would be nice to check the list from my phone too."
- **Who:** Laura Gómez, product owner
- **When:** 2026-08-20
- **Status:** out-of-scope (decision: version 1 is a local command-line tool; phone access is revisited after v1 ships — Laura Gómez, 2026-08-22)
